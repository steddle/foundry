## Where we stand

Steddle sells the claimant side of a collective action as a running service. Four steps: find the case, build the claimant platform, book the claimants, and hand a funded matter to counsel as an operation rather than a list.

The positioning is fixed and the wording is deliberate. Towards funders the work is called *origination*. Towards a court that word is avoided: a fee per claimant reads as an introduction commission under the WAMCA, so the register is described by what it proves, not by what it costs.

The structural claim is the formula, and it is the only part of this that nobody else sells:

- **The register lives with Steddle.** Claimants register on our platform, under our terms, from day one.
- **The authority lives with the foundation.** Per matter, a notarial deed records delivery of the in-scope records and the foundation's unconditional access to them.
  - The deed is the foundation's proof that it reaches its own class.
  - The objection register is annexed to it.
- **The services stay with Steddle.** Notice, opt-out and opt-in administration, and claimant communications in every language the class speaks.

Every piece of that exists somewhere in the market. The bundle does not.

## The register

### What a record holds

A record is a person, a claim and the evidence for it, checked before it is counted. A record that fails a check stays in the register with the reason, so the count a court sees is the count that held.

1. The claimant, verified by email and, where the matter needs it, by a document.
2. The claim: the vehicle, the purchase, the period.
3. The evidence, uploaded and graded.

> A register is only as good as the reason it can give for every record it refused.

## What comes next

Three things decide whether the model holds beyond the first matter: the deed, the second matter, and the price a funder pays for a verified record. The deed is with the notary. The second matter is in [the pipeline](https://steddle.com). The price is the open question, and the rest of this briefing is about it.

### The price of a record

A funder pays for what a record saves it, not for the record. On the first matter that saving is the cost of a class the foundation can prove it reaches, which is the certification risk itself.
