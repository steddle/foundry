## Four recommendations

### 1. Measure and report take-up, not only notice

The regime tests whether notice was given, not whether money was claimed. We recommend that the Tribunal require, at certification, a stated response-rate target, and that the class representative report the actual rate at distribution.

### 2. Make claim websites verifiable as genuine

A link alone does not overcome a consumer's reasonable fear of a scam, which is the real barrier to claiming. We recommend a recognised verification standard for claim websites.

### 3. Weigh claimant-side quality at certification

A provable, reachable class is the most effective filter against speculative claims, and the best protection against the low take-up and fraud the distribution proposals target.
